# Research scenes review plan: what was seen in the client

Looking at the glTF research set's extended scenes in the client, after the research scenes plan's steps (docs/research-scenes-plan.md), showed eleven artifacts: jagged teeth on a box's top edge (cornell-box), speckled noise in the bathroom's mirror, square voxel-sized patches with sharp edges (classroom, veach-ajar), caustics that settle slowly and look wrong after a camera move (cornell-caustic), indirect light that settles slowly with moving splotches (kitchen, living-room, veach-bidir, classroom, veach-ajar), and bright single-pixel speckles (lamp, modern-hall, spaceship). They come from three causes. Status: steps 1 to 5 are done, step 6 is not started, and what is left is small (below).

## What they are: three causes

### A. The emitters' caustic photons (lamp, modern-hall, spaceship, cornell-caustic)

With the set's conditions (`SPEEDO_SOLID_TRANSMISSION=1`) every glass surface counts as solid, so the diffuse's shadow rays stopped at all of it and the photons carried all the light that passed through glass. The photons are few per pixel, so the light through glass that doesn't focus (a lamp's emitter inside its bulb, wall lights behind a flat rail, red lights inside a dome) was speckled grain where a shadow ray would have given the right light. In cornell-caustic the places the screen hides and a refraction shows were lit by coarse world cells sized by the eye's distance, which were grainy after a move and started over at new sizes.

### B. The surface cache's finest entries show (cornell-box, bathroom, classroom, veach-ajar)

The blocks and teeth are where a pixel's lookup leaves some of its 8 voxels out (the sight test, the far sides rule, the facing rule) and takes the others by their weights' sum, so the light steps from one kept voxel to the next. They are gone with `SPEEDO_GI_CACHE=0`. Several sub-causes, each found separately:

- The facing rule and `GiFarSides`' clause for a voxel behind a single sided surface left out too many voxels where a large voxel holds several surfaces (the classroom's walls).
- Where a surface has no voxel of its own, the air beside it mixed the light of every surface around it.
- At cornell-box's box edge, voxels whose anchors start past the face found the top, and rays heading into that surface were lit by the lamp.
- The bathroom's mirror shows a wall that is off the screen, whose blocks no pixel's lookup stamps, and whose emitter light a reflection's hit took from one voxel.
- The scenes' floors and backdrops are far larger than the room, so the finest clip level is coarse there. More clip levels were tried against the research set and not kept: they changed almost nothing, and moved the classroom away from its reference.

### C. The indirect light settles slowly (kitchen, living-room, veach-bidir, classroom, veach-ajar)

What settled slowly was not noise but the light's level: the bounces build up one after the other from a cold start, with the probes' share of a voxel's light, blocks no lookup reads, the coarser levels' turns and the bounce's start delay each adding to it. The cache's averaging window, counted in updates rather than samples, also made more rays no help. The averaging is set for a still scene's noise, not for a cold start. veach-bidir's left wall is noise that averages out slowly, since few rays find the small bright patch that lights it.

## The plan

Judged at the reports' poses at 3840x2160, against the research comparison (`scripts/researchcompare.ps1`) and the indirect lighting's regression scenes (Sponza's columns, the bathroom's bench, Bistro, CornellBox), by eye, and by cost at the target (1440p balanced).

1. Light through glass that doesn't focus goes straight (A): done. The rule is decided at each shadow ray, by the faces it meets (a slab or shell, or flat faces), not per glass instance (`EmitterGatheredByGlass`, docs/notes/rendering.md, "The emitters' caustics"); the photons follow the same rule. The cost is the transmissive fragment shader's size.
2. The caustics' density, estimated as photon mapping does (A): done. Photons are kept in the world in cells of three sizes, a lookup takes the coarsest level and each finer one by the photons it holds, so the picture is smooth from the start, sharpens as photons arrive, and survives a camera move. Not done: moving the hits' light sample out of the transmissive fragment shader (docs/backlog.md).
3. The cache's lookups continuous (B): done in parts. Kept: the facing rule in the GI Sides pass's masks (`GiSidesMaskFacing`), the far sides clause not taken for a voxel with a drawn side facing the lookup's surface (`GiFarSides`), air voxels taking light from the surfaces facing each face's way (`ComputeGiCacheFilter`, `kGiSpreadFacedLeast`), and a fix in `GiCacheUpdate` for rays heading into a surface found past a voxel's own face. Tried and dropped: a fill of the left out voxels, a jittered lookup, a wider fetch, a whole lookup from the surface's own voxels, more clip levels. docs/notes/indirect-lighting.md, "A voxel's light is taken only where it was gathered". Left: soft notches along living-room's arches, and faint teeth on cornell-box's front face.
4. The reflections' hits take the kept light interpolated (B): done, differently than planned. Reflection hits stamp the blocks they read (`GiMarkSeenHit`), `TracedEmitterKept` takes the 4 columns around a hit by their shares in the clips that light it, and a hit hidden by the screen takes the voxels' light instead of a light sample of its own (`TracedEmitterHidden`). Left out for its cost: stamps at the transmissive fragments' rays' hits (docs/backlog.md).
5. A cold start that settles in about a second (C): done. A settled entry is one whose window is full now (`ComputeGiCacheFilter`), every block is traced and filtered and every clip updated every frame for a cache's first updates (`kGiCacheColdFrameCount`), no bounce for the first frames, and the cache's window counts rays rather than updates (`GiClipClock::raysSinceCacheReset`). The cold start costs more while it lasts, accepted. The warm-up's ray count is an option, View > Rendering Features > Global Illumination... > Warm-Up Rays (`SPEEDO_GI_CACHE_WARM_RAYS`, 4 by default); a specialization constant for it was measured and gave nothing.
6. Small bright places sampled by importance (C): not started. The probes' and the cache's rays would be aimed at what is bright in their view and weighed by that density. A voxel's entry has no picture of what is bright around it, and the probes keep irradiance, not radiance, so the way that would work is a list of lit voxels sampled by power with a shadow ray to one of them. More rays while cold would also help the first seconds but cost too much in the heavier scenes.

Also fixed on the way: spaceship's specks around its display were the traced refraction's rough lobe ray meeting a bright bar in some frames. While the view is still, a rough surface's refraction light is now the mean of many frames' rays, shared with the neighbouring pixels (`GlassLight`, `kTransmittedLobeShare`, the Glass History pass; docs/notes/rendering.md). While the view moves the lobe's grain is as before (docs/backlog.md, rough glass).

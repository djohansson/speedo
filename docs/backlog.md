- **The thorough voxel test for the reflections' near field.** The hits' voxel choice is thorough (`GiHitVoxel<kThorough>`: other surfaces' voxels tested too, not only those marked apart) for the main pass's near field and the surface cache's and the probes' rays. Try it for the reflections' near field (`GiNearTrace<false>`), and keep it if it clearly improves the picture for a modest cost; what the cache's and the probes' rays' test costs a frame is not measured yet. docs/notes/indirect-lighting.md, "One rule for every lookup and hit".
- **The light the voxels' albedo was adding.** A hit now reflects by its own triangle's albedo (`GiHitShare`), where it reflected by its voxel's, the most of what wrote the voxel. Interiors come out darker and further from their references (the research set's classroom scenes most): light that is missing elsewhere and was covered by that. Find where it goes. docs/notes/indirect-lighting.md, "The cache's rays' hits take the lights' light at the hit".
- **A voxel's surface chosen by coverage.** Deferred. The voxelizer keeps one surface a voxel, chosen by the order of its normal's bits, so at a coarse level something smaller than a voxel can take a voxel that is mostly another surface, and a wall meeting others in a corner can lose its voxels there (a dark line in bathroom2's niche). Choosing by coverage would cost the voxelizing on every scroll and every frame for what moves; keeping the frontmost surface instead brought up other artifacts. Revisit if such a corner shows. docs/notes/indirect-lighting.md, "The niche behind the statuettes".
- **A surface with no voxel of its own beside a corner.** Where a column of voxels holds another surface (the side of a cabinet standing against a wall), the wall's strip in it has none of its own: its lookups leave those voxels out as facing another way (`GiSidesMaskFacing`) and take what is left of their 8 at full weight, the voxels beyond. The lookups now read a voxel along the surface where that holds one of their own (`GiSidesStep`); what is left is where none is within a voxel, and a traced ray's hit with no voxel to stand for it, whose lookup has neither rule nor a mask (`TracedIndirect`; a hit otherwise reads one voxel, `GiHitSample`). In the research set's bathroom a faint lighter patch is left on the wall under the window sill's end beside the vanity, and a lighter bit a finer voxel wide on the sill's front under the window casing's foot: the finer level's lookup has nothing left there, and the coarser one's takes the foot's voxel, which sees the point and holds the light of the sill's top. Tried and not kept: leaving out the voxels behind a left out voxel's plane too (new blocks at the sill and a notch at the skirting), and a second lookup a voxel along the surface toward the voxels the point sees (it lands in brighter voxels as often as the wall's own). What it needs is the wall's own light at such a point: from the wall's nearest own voxels chosen by what they hold (surface, facing and plane), not by position. docs/notes/indirect-lighting.md, "A voxel's light is taken only where it was gathered".

## Glass and caustics

- **The emitters' caustics beyond the views.** Photons land only in the cells of what the views hold. What is out of every view takes the voxels' light straight through glass (a coarse level of cells for every landing would fix it), and so do the indirect lighting's voxels (the cells could hold their light, bouncing the caustics). Highlights through solid glass are the direct light's, since photons carry irradiance alone (a direction kept with it would let the specular use them). cornell-caustic's ceiling is darker than its reference's. docs/notes/rendering.md, "The emitters' caustics".
- **Refraction hits don't stamp the cache's blocks.** Reflection hits stamp the blocks they read (`GiMarkSeenHit`); the transmissive fragments' rays don't, since the stamps made that shader much slower by its size. A pass of its own for those stamps, or the next item, would allow it.
- **The traced hits' light sample out of the transmissive fragment shader.** A refraction's hit hidden from the screen takes a light sample (`TracedEmitterHidden`), and the rule telling glass that gathers light from glass that doesn't made the shader slower by its size alone. Direct light kept in cells of the world, as the caustics are, sampled in a compute pass for the cells the hits ask for, would take both out of it. docs/notes/rendering.md, "The emitters' caustics".
- **glass-of-water at 60 Hz.** The one research scene over the frame budget, most of it Main Transmissive: its glass, water, ice and bubbles refract through up to `SHADER_TYPES_CAUSTIC_SURFACE_COUNT` surfaces each. docs/research-scenes-plan.md, "Where it stands".
- **Rough glass closer to its references.** Step 6's lobe ray improved it, but lamp's frosted bulb still shows a thin dark rim, and past roughness 0.15 most of a rough surface's light is the transmission texture's mips: a blur by roughness alone, not by how far behind the surface the scene is. Next: the nearest glass layer's reflection and refraction each a lobe ray a pixel, filtered and accumulated by Reflection passes of their own, as the visibility buffer's glossy reflections are (without the filter it went wrong). A still view already averages up to 128 frames' rays (`GlassLight`); while the view moves it is one frame's ray, so a history reprojected by what is behind the glass would be the first part. Judge by lamp's bulb, bathroom2's jar and TransmissionRoughnessTest. docs/research-scenes-plan.md, C2 and step 6; docs/notes/rendering.md, "Traced reflections and refractions".

## Testing and performance

- **Visual testing's open steps**: a reference for the indirect lighting (likely a progressive path tracer), golden images on lavapipe in CI, more known answers, CI additions; open calls: where stored references live, CI's time budget, which reference. docs/visual-testing-plan.md.
- **visualtest's traced-shadows fails on its first run after a shader change.** The reference view (`traced-shadow-reference`) comes out without the ground's shadows when its client runs while the kernels are compiled cold, and the check fails by about 0.16 stops blurred, with the BVHs' traversal and with ray queries (whose ray-queries check fails then too); run again, with the kernels cached, both pass. The check's two views trace only where the shadow atlas isn't sure, so the likely cause is the atlas: tiles drawn and kept while their pipelines are being made, as the shadow-atlas check once found. To find: which pipeline the reference's 8 frames don't wait for.
- **GI Sides' cost.** The pass keeps a pixel's masks from frame to frame and traces only those the last frame doesn't hold (`ComputeGiSides`). Left of it: a pixel whose lookup crossed into other voxels with no pixel beside it there traces at once, and makes its thread group trace with it, so the groups pay for several times the rays traced (a wider search, or such pixels' masks made in a pass of their own, would spare that); a kept mask is the one made at another jitter of the pixel's point, so along thin things (a window's casing) the masks are no longer averaged over the frames' jitters, a little more speckle there; and the pass's kernel without anything kept is slower than the one before it. Tried and not kept: a mask shared among the pixels of a thread group (the threads that wait cost as much as those that trace); a thread group's threads kept busy (a group paid only about a third more than its pixels' rays: the cost was the rays); and the sight test's point taken once a voxel, as a mask kept per voxel would have it (the lines up the corners and along what stands on a wall come back: the test needs the pixel's own point). The same rays where a hit's voxel is chosen (`GiHitVoxel`'s `GiApexSees`) are the rest of what the lookups cost (the next entry); the traced rays' hits read one voxel instead of making masks (`GiHitSample`). docs/notes/indirect-lighting.md, "A voxel's light is taken only where it was gathered".
- **The sight rays where a hit's voxel is chosen.** A hit takes the voxel that stands for it only where that voxel's gathering point sees it (`GiHitVoxel`'s `GiApexSees`, for every candidate marked apart, in each clip asked), and in a scene of small things nearly every voxel is marked: in the research set's classroom those rays are most of the Reflection Trace pass and of the near field. They can't simply go: without them the picture is several of 255 brighter, the light of what is behind a hit's surface. Tried and not kept: a voxel accepted without a ray where the hit lies on the plane its light was gathered on (few of the traced voxels are the hit's own surface's: almost nothing saved); a voxel of a surface facing another way than the hit's left out without a ray (classroom's frame a sixth less, but more hits have no voxel then and take the lookup without masks: the window's casing in the bathroom's mirror lit up again); and that with the lookup's voxels chosen by what they hold where no voxel stands for the hit (`GiSidesMaskFacing` without its rays: the bathroom as it was, but its frame a little slower than before, classroom's a tenth less). The marks' own rays in the cache's filter are a few tenths of a millisecond a frame: not worth keeping between frames. Since then the tests are a mask a kernel can be compiled without, a pass at a time (`SPEEDO_GI_HIT_SKIP` and its like), which showed where the cost and the leak are: the leak comes in through the probes' and the cache's rays, which keep every test, and the traced rays' hits no longer look at the voxels beside or ask the coarser clips (`kGiHitTraced`). Left to try that way: the marked voxels' ray in the traced rays' hits, most of what they still cost in classroom, which lights the window's casing in the bathroom's mirror when left out for every hit (by the hit's distance, or by what the ray came from, it might not); and the final gather's rays, whose marked and far side tests share a ray, so that only leaving both out saves it, and classroom comes out brighter. What remains otherwise is the reflections' ray budget (`SPEEDO_REFLECTION_STRIDE`: a ray every pixel with ray queries; every second pixel each way is a third less of classroom's frame and a sixth of the bathroom's, the still picture close): the user's call. docs/notes/indirect-lighting.md, "One rule for every lookup and hit".
- **The forward fragment kernels' size.** On KosmicKrisp, where NIR inlines every call, `FragmentMain` and `FragmentTransparent` are each a Metal function too large for Metal's compiler service to compile two of at once, and a cold start compiles them for minutes. Graphics pipelines are created one at a time there meanwhile (`DeviceLimits::graphicsPipelinesSerial`), and after a change to those shaders the quick tier's first run is at its clients' timeout (ClearCoatTest on Vulkan, several clients compiling at once). Shrink them: the shading and the traced rays are about half each, and the lookups (`GiSampleAt`), the hits' voxel choice, the BVH traversal and the sky are each inlined at many call sites; the reference view could leave the forward shaders. Measure with `MESA_KK_DEBUG=msl` and `SPEEDO_LOG_PIPELINES=1`. docs/notes/layering.md, "Pipelines made without waiting".
- **Performance**: static casters' shadow maps cached (virtual shadow maps longer term), and rich worlds: model instances, streaming and level of detail, async compute, recording on several threads, quality presets aimed at a frame time. docs/performance-plan.md.
